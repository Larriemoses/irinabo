# Demonstration script

Use this order for the submission video. Keep the entire story focused on report provenance and accountable follow-up.

1. State that Unity Transit Demo, its passengers, staff and incidents are fictional.
2. Open Judge Mode briefly and explain that the product labels capabilities as LIVE, SIMULATED, FICTIONAL or DEFERRED.
3. Open journey `TW204` and show the passenger journey link/QR flow.
4. If the Twilio Sandbox has been successfully tested end to end, show a short **text** report through WhatsApp. Otherwise use the labelled browser demonstration and say clearly that it is simulated.
5. Show the protected-report decision: the report involves transport staff, so ordinary garage staff must not receive the protected case.
6. Open protected case `IRN-204-031`.
7. Compare the passenger and driver source accounts side by side. Say: “IrinAbo preserves both accounts; AI does not decide which person is telling the truth.”
8. Show the two demonstration location sources and point out source/freshness labels. Do not claim the WhatsApp snapshot was ingested live unless that feature has actually been added and tested.
9. Show the accountability distinction: notification is not acceptance. Accept responsibility and record the next action in the authenticated workspace where available.
10. Show the server-side escalation concept. State that the external call is simulated in this proof of concept.
11. Open `/receipt/demo-receipt` and choose “This is not resolved” to demonstrate append-only passenger feedback.
12. End on Judge Mode and restate the capability labels.

Do not claim:
- guaranteed rescue or emergency response;
- a live public-agency integration;
- live voice-note transcription;
- live WhatsApp current-location ingestion;
- continuous background tracking;
- that a browser demo report was persisted when it was not.
