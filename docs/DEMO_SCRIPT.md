# IrinAbo demo script

Target length: 2 minutes.

## 0:00–0:15 · Problem

Show the homepage.

> When a passenger reports a problem during a road journey, the message can lose its source, reach the wrong person or disappear without accountable follow-up. IrinAbo keeps the report attached to the journey and shows who accepted the next action.

State that every person, company and incident shown is fictional.

## 0:15–0:45 · Passenger report

Open `/report`.

1. Keep the seeded concern.
2. Continue to the routing question.
3. Choose **Yes, staff are involved**.
4. Send the demo report.

> The message reaches the demo API. A deterministic rule locks it to the protected route. The screen clearly says that shared persistence and external notification are not enabled for this public simulator.

## 0:45–1:15 · Evidence without a truth score

Open `/judge/case`.

> The passenger and driver accounts remain side by side. IrinAbo does not choose which person is telling the truth. It preserves the source, marks the location channel and freshness, and records the routing history.

Point to the stale web location and active WhatsApp snapshot.

## 1:15–1:35 · Journey access

Open `/journey/TW204`.

> A passenger can join with a browser link, choose when to share a location and enter the WhatsApp Sandbox flow. Browser sharing stops when the page closes, so the interface does not promise continuous tracking.

Do not grant location permission during the recording unless you intend to show a deliberately shared test location.

## 1:35–1:50 · Accountability receipt

Open `/receipt/demo-receipt` and select **This is not resolved**.

> A recorded action is not automatically a resolved outcome. The passenger can request another review.

## 1:50–2:00 · Honest capability labels

Finish at `/judge`.

> Judge Mode separates what is live, simulated, fictional and deferred. IrinAbo is a working proof of concept, not an emergency service.

## Recording checklist

- Record at 1080p with the browser zoom at 90–100%.
- Hide bookmarks, notifications and personal accounts.
- Keep the cursor still while speaking.
- Use the public routes above. Do not rely on staff credentials.
- Export MP4, MOV, WebM or AVI below 250 MB.
- Check that every label remains readable on a phone screen.
