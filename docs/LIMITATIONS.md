# Prototype limitations

- WhatsApp and voice delivery are simulated until credentials and consenting test recipients are configured.
- Browsers cannot guarantee location after a page is hidden, frozen, locked, or closed.
- Device location can be inaccurate or spoofed.
- AI provides suggestions; it does not decide truth, access, protection, or resolution.
- No public emergency agency, hospital, police service, or road-safety body is integrated.
- Unity Transit Demo, all incidents, and the independent safety desk are fictional.
- Nigeria is the first configuration pack, not a claim of legal or operational uniformity.
