# Trust model

IrinAbo describes evidence rather than awarding a universal truth badge. Original reports are immutable, corrections append a linked record, and copied messages do not become independent sources. Driver and passenger accounts retain equal source visibility.

`Sent`, `delivered`, `accepted`, `action underway`, and `closed with outcome` are distinct. A connected call does not mean responsibility was accepted. Coordinates display source, capture time, receipt time, accuracy when known, and freshness.

Protected status is a fixed routing and visibility choice, not a truth judgment. A protected report cannot be downgraded by a client or AI, and an organisation admin does not inherit protected access without a safety assignment.
